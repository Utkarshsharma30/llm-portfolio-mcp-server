### 1\. Taj Mahal

No wonder you already know this place! Being one of the seven wonders of the world, Taj Mahal attracts tourists from all over the globe. It is also one of the significant places to visit in Agra considering its glorious history. It is believed that Shah Jahan built this monument to showcase his love for Mumtaz. ![Check out the best places to visit in Agra](https://www.treebo.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Taj-mahal.jpg)

*Taj Mahal*

Taj Mahal is surrounded by green gardens and it does take a good amount of time to reach the interiors.

Be prepared for queues as people travel to witness the beautiful ornamentation and intricate details of the mausoleum. It also has a rectangular pool with fountains, elevating the beauty of this place. Celebrated for its perfect symmetry, Taj Mahal is definitely one of the must-visit tourist attractions in Agra.

### 2\. Agra Fort

Imagine the feeling of witnessing the glorious past of the Mughal Empire. Agra Fort surely takes you deep into the historic power and prestige of the Mughal emperors. It is believed that many emperors have resided in the fort. Interestingly, it is one of the best places to visit in Agra, which is also a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

![Check out the best places to visit in Agra](https://www.treebo.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Agra-fort.jpg)

*Agra Fort*

As you enter this place, all you can see are detailed carvings, stunning construction and intricate interiors. What’s more? This massive fort is home to multiple spots, including Diwan-i-Am and Diwan-i-Khas. While one was used for public appearances, the other was purely for private audiences. You can also see the Jasmine Tower holding its glory to date.

Anguri Bagh is another important attraction in the fort. Known as the grape garden, it is built in a Mughal-era style. It is also an ideal spot for photography considering its top-notch maintenance. It takes around 3 hours to take a complete tour of this fort, so keep yourself free for this.

**Location:** Bijli Ghar, Near, Agra Fort Crossing, Pipal Mandi, Mantola, Agra, Uttar Pradesh 282002

### 3\. Itmad-ud-Daula

Each monument in this city is a precious structure that takes you back to history. That’s exactly why you must visit the mausoleum of Itmad-ud-Daula. It is one of the popular places to visit in Agra for its stunning architecture and intricate designs. Interestingly, the tomb was constructed by the daughter of Itmad-ud-Daula named Nur Jahan.

![Check out the best places to visit in Agra](https://www.treebo.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Itmad-ud-Daula.jpg)

Itmad-ud-Daula

What’s more? In the past, this tomb was one of the very first structures made completely with white marble. No wonder it showcases the inspiring craftsmanship of people back then. On the other hand, the mausoleum stands as a reminder of a daughter’s love and power for her father. Lush gardens, royal interiors and serenity are all that you get to see here.

**Location:** Moti Bagh, Agra, Uttar Pradesh 282006

### 4\. Gurudwara Guru Ka Taal

Just as you think of Gurudwara, it is obvious to think of the famous [Golden Temple](https://www.treebo.com/blog/reasons-you-must-visit-the-golden-temple-in-amritsar/). However, Gurudwara Guru Ka Taal in Agra is also an important pilgrimage site for Sikhs. It is one of the sacred places to visit in Agra for seeking blessings. It was originally built as a reservoir; however, Guru Tegh Bahadur selected this place for religious persecution.

![Check out the best places to visit in Agra](https://www.treebo.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Gurudwara-Guru-Ka-Taal.jpg)

Gurudwara Guru Ka Taal

In today’s time, you can visit this place to feel a sense of contentment and peace. Surrounded by lush green gardens, it is a great destination for meditation and self-reflection. It is the aura of devotion that makes you feel incredibly comfortable. Do not forget to witness the awe-inspiring architecture attracting thousands of devotees every year.

**Location:** Chennai – Delhi Hwy, Maharishi Puram Colony, Agra, Uttar Pradesh 282007

### 5\. Tomb of Akbar

Imagine the glory of Emperor Akbar, who himself built a tomb for his afterlife. The Tomb of Akbar is one of the iconic places to visit in Agra. It is a great destination to understand the majesty of Akbar. He specifically selected the neighbourhood of Sikandra due to its peaceful atmosphere. The area is away from the hustle and bustle of city life.

![Check out the best places to visit in Agra](https://www.treebo.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Tomb-of-akbar.jpg)

Tomb of Akbar

Construction of the tomb began in 1600; however, it was completed by Akbar’s son in 1613. Initially, the tomb was filled with jewels, beautiful paintings and precious items. However, it was destroyed and looted during British rule. In today’s time, the elegant tomb redefines fine craftsmanship and artistry. You get to see intricate designs on the red sandstone. Lastly, it is one of the famous tourist attractions in Agra for serenity seekers.

**Location:** Tomb of Akbar The Great Area, Sikandra, Agra, Uttar Pradesh 282007

### 6\. Jama Masjid

Your trip remains incomplete without visiting the Jama Masjid. Being one of the famous places to visit in Agra, it is a monument of pride and beauty. You shall find multiple constructions by Shah Jahan. The mosque is one of them. It was specifically dedicated to showcasing love for his daughter, Jahanara Begum.

![Check out the best places to visit in Agra](https://www.treebo.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Jama-Masjid.jpg)

Jama Masjid

What makes it one of the unique tourist places in Agra is its architecture. Jama Masjid was once compared to Baitul-Mamur (a jewel-studded mosque believed to be present in heaven). You can witness the usage of red sandstone through the mosque. A large courtyard, fountains, arched pillars and a massive prayer hall define this place of worship. ### 7\. Mehtab Bagh

Mehtab Bagh is one of the best places to visit in Agra for a lovely time. As the name suggests, it is a Mughal-style inspired garden. Located on the back side of the Taj Mahal, you get to see the Yamuna River flowing swiftly. The garden is best known for its well-manicured lanes, floral beauty and lush greenery.

Mehtab Bagh

Interestingly, the place is less crowded for you to admire the beauty of Taj Mahal. You can take a romantic stroll across the river. Moreover, it is a great place for photography and sunset viewing. It is best to visit Mehtab Bagh on the full moon day, as the glory of the moon reflects on the white Taj Mahal. ### 8\. Shri Mankameshwar Temple

Mythological narrations truly elevate the beauty of a place. Shri Mankameshwar Temple is indeed one of the sacred places to visit in Agra for a divine time. Dedicated to Lord Shiva, it is believed that Lord himself created the Shiva Linga here. It is connected to the birth of Lord Krishna.

![Check out the best places to visit in Agra](https://www.treebo.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Shri-Mankameshwar-temple.jpg)

Shri Mankameshwar Temple

As per the legend, Lord Shiva wished to make Krishna play in his lap. However, his mother was shocked by the appearance of Lord Shiva. Later, Krishna pointed towards him and urged him to play in his lap. That’s when Lord Shiva constructed a linga at the Mankameshwar temple. That’s exactly why it is one of the famous tourist attractions in Agra.

What’s more? Locals and tourists gather to perform aarti during mornings and evenings. The aura is filled with positive energy and vibrations. Even you can feel a sense of fulfilment by visiting this place. Make sure to opt for traditional Indian attire, as it is well-appreciated here.

**Location:** Daresi Rd, Rawatpara, Sheb Bazar, Mantola, Agra, Uttar Pradesh 282003

### 9\. Jahangir Mahal

Jahangir Mahal definitely needs a special mention when it comes to this city! Being one of the famous places to visit in Agra, this mahal is located inside the massive Agra fort. You can enter from the Amar Singh Gate to reach this place conveniently. Did you know the mahal was specifically dedicated to the wives of Akbar? It was a glorious place for women where to pray and relax.

![Check out the best places to visit in Agra](https://www.treebo.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Jahangir-mahal.jpg)

Jahangir Mahal

Later on, the mahal was used by Jahangir as his private residence. You can witness a blend of red sandstone and white marble redefining the architectural glory. Notice the carved walls, vintage balconies and huge gardens elevating the richness of the mahal.

What’s more? You must see Hauz-i-Jahangiri, which was used for bathing. It is believed to be carved from a single stone. In the early times, rose water was stored in this tank for freshening. So, get ready to dive into Mughal history as you walk through the stories of royalty. You can easily book [hotels in Rakabganj, Agra](https://www.treebo.com/hotels-in-rakabganj-agra/) for a comfortable stay.

**Location:** 52HF+37V, Rakabganj, Fort, Agra, Uttar Pradesh 282003

### 10\. Chini Ka Rauza

The city is home to multiple monuments that lost their glory with time. Chini ka Rauza is one of them! However, if you are a history buff and wish to explore the architecture, this is the place to be. Chini ka Rauza is basically the tomb of Allama Afzal Khan Mullah, prime minister of Shah Jahan. The structure has been celebrated for its Persian-style construction.

![Check out the best places to visit in Agra](https://www.treebo.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Chini-ka-Rauza.jpg)

Chini ka Rauza

In today’s time, Chini Ka Rauza remains one of the underrated places to visit in Agra. As you visit this place, you can still see the intricate tiles and colourful designs on them. The craftsmanship is what draws art lovers here! Even after fading over time, the monument stands as a testament to awe-inspiring beauty and artistry.

**Location:** Katra Wazir Khan, Agra, Uttar Pradesh 282006

You literally cannot go home without buying authentic pethas (a sweet dish) from the city. Sadar Bazar is one of the popular places to visit in Agra for a wonderful shopping time. You can find everything in this market, ranging from traditional handicrafts and embroidered bags to showpieces created with marble.

![Check out the best places to visit in Agra](https://www.treebo.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Sadar-Bazar-.jpg)

Sadar Bazar

As you walk through the buzzing area, you can find multiple leather products, including bags, belts and footwear. Interestingly, the collection here is vibrant enough to be gifted as souvenirs for your loved ones. So, energise yourself to navigate through the narrow lanes of the market.

**Location:** Sadar Bhatti Road, Agra Cantt, Idgah Colony, Agra, Uttar Pradesh 282001

## Things To Know Before Visiting Agra

- Most monuments are crowded during the daytime. It is best to arrive in the early morning to avoid the rush.
- It is best to wear comfortable clothing, as most places require a good amount of walking.
- While most tourist attractions in Agra look gorgeous, they are actually places of religious significance. Hence, it is important to choose modest clothing while visiting these places.
- Avoid unauthorised guides, as they might loot you without giving the right information. Always check their IDs or certifications before.
- Negotiate with public transport, including rickshaws, as they might quote a higher price initially.
- You are certain to find multiple sellers across every tourist place. It is best to deny them kindly and keep moving ahead.
