From rolling tea plantations to sun-kissed beaches, Kerala offers a diverse tapestry of experiences. Every destination here is a perfect blend of nature, history, and adventure. Let’s take a look at the top 20 places you absolutely must visit!

## 1\. Munnar – A Hill Station Retreat Amidst Tea Gardens

If there's one place in Kerala that manages to capture the heart of every traveller irrespective of their interests, it's Munnar. Embraced by sprawling tea plantations, cool mountain breeze and breathtaking scenery, this dreamy hill station sits at an altitude of 1,600 meters above sea level.

Whether you're a honeymooner looking for romance, a nature lover seeking serenity, or an adventure enthusiast craving excitement, Munnar welcomes you with its misty hills, lush green valleys, and soothing breeze.

### Things to Do in Munnar, Kerala:

- Visit the Tea Museum to learn about tea processing
- Explore the Eravikulam National Park and spot Nilgiri Tahr
- Take a scenic drive to Top Station for panoramic views
- Enjoy boating at Kundala Lake

## 2\. Alleppey & Kumarakom – The Backwaters Paradise

Alleppey (Alappuzha) and Kumarakom are famous for their enchanting backwaters, making them among the best tourist places in Kerala. A houseboat cruise through these serene waters is an experience you should not miss. The labyrinth of canals, coconut groves, and floating villages create an unforgettable setting, ideal for relaxation and rejuvenation.

### Things to Do in Alleppey & Kumarakom, Kerala:

- Stay overnight on a luxury houseboat
- Visit the Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary
- Experience the traditional Snake Boat Race (if visiting during Onam)
- Enjoy a relaxing Ayurvedic spa by the backwaters

## 3\. Wayanad – A Blend of Waterfalls and Wildlife

Wayanad, nestled in the Western Ghats, is a dream destination for nature lovers and adventure seekers. With its lush forests, misty hills, and rich wildlife, it’s a top pick for those exploring tourist places in Kerala. The region boasts charming villages, spice plantations, and cultural sites that make it a perfect mix of relaxation and exploration.

### Things to Do in Wayanad, Kerala:

- Trek to the Edakkal Caves to see ancient carvings
- Visit the Meenmutty and Soochipara waterfalls
- Explore the Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary
- Camp near Banasura Sagar Dam, India’s largest earthen dam

## 4\. Thekkady – Home to Periyar National Park

Thekkady is one of Kerala's most fascinating tourist destinations for those who love animals. The Periyar National Park is an exciting place to visit because of its rich biodiversity and lush forests. The area is a sensory joy and is well-known for its fragrant spice crops.

### Things to Do in Thekkady, Kerala:

- Enjoy a boat safari in Periyar Lake to spot elephants and tigers
- Take a guided nature walk through the Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary
- Experience bamboo rafting for an adventurous touch
- Visit the spice plantations for an aromatic experience

## 5\. Vagamon – Offbeat Hill Station and Camping Delight

If you are looking for an offbeat experience, Vagamon is an underrated yet stunning hill station in Kerala. Nestled amidst lush greenery, mist-covered valleys, and rolling meadows, it offers a perfect blend of adventure and tranquility. Vagamon maintains its natural beauty in contrast to the more commercialised hill stations, which makes it the perfect retreat for hikers, nature enthusiasts, and anyone looking for tranquilly away from the bustle of the city.

### Things to Do in Vagamon, Kerala:

- Try paragliding for a bird’s-eye view of the valley
- Visit the Pine Forests for a serene escape
- Experience camping under the stars with Exoticamp
- Explore the scenic Vagamon Meadows and Tea Estates

Enjoy breathtaking views of the sunrise and clean mountain air as you awaken. ## 6\. Kochi – The Cultural Capital of Kerala

Cochin, another name for Kochi, is one of Kerala's most popular tourist destinations, fusing modernism with a wealth of historical influences. The city is charming because of its colonial architecture, vibrant marketplaces, breathtaking coastline, and intriguing blend of British, Portuguese, and Dutch traditions.

### Things to Do in Kochi, Kerala:

- Visit Fort Kochi for its Portuguese-style buildings
- Watch a Kathakali dance performance
- Explore the Jewish Synagogue and Mattancherry Palace
- Enjoy a scenic sunset at Marine Drive
- Try authentic Kerala cuisine at a local restaurant

## 7\. Gavi – An Eco-Tourism Haven

With its unspoiled forests, stunning scenery, and tranquil environment, Gavi is a hidden treasure among Kerala's tourist destinations. This ecotourism destination, which is part of the Periyar Tiger Reserve, is ideal for adventurers and nature lovers who want to get away from the bustle of the city.

### Things to Do in Gavi, Kerala:

- Take a jeep safari through the dense forests
- Go for a nature trek to spot exotic flora and fauna
- Enjoy a boat ride in Gavi Lake
- Visit the Sabarimala viewpoint for mesmerizing views

## 8\. Varkala – A Beach Lover’s Paradise

For beach enthusiasts, Varkala is one of Kerala's top tourist destinations. Varkala is a distinctive and beautiful location that stands out from other Keralan beaches due to its striking red laterite cliffs that border the Arabian Sea. It's the ideal retreat for backpackers, couples, and single people because of its lively cafes, peaceful beaches, and spiritual atmosphere.

### Things to Do in Varkala, Kerala:

- Relax at Varkala Beach, also known as Papanasam Beach
- Take a dip in the natural spring, believed to have medicinal properties
- Visit the ancient Janardanaswamy Temple
- Explore the cafés and beach shacks for a laid-back vibe

## 9\. Bekal – A Coastal Gem with a Historic Fort

Bekal, located in northern Kerala, is famous for its Bekal Fort, one of the largest and best-preserved forts in the state. TWith its enormous walls and keyhole-shaped structure, the fort provides stunning sweeping vistas of the Arabian Sea and has served as a backdrop for a number of Bollywood productions. Bekal is the perfect place for history aficionados, beach lovers, and photographers because of its tranquil atmosphere and historical significance.

### Things to Do in Bekal, Kerala:

- Explore the Bekal Fort and its scenic surroundings
- Relax at Bekal Beach
- Visit the Chandragiri Fort for historical insights
- Enjoy a houseboat ride through the Valiyaparamba backwaters

## 10\. Athirapally Falls – The Niagara of India

No list of tourist places in Kerala is complete without mentioning Athirapally Falls, the largest waterfall in Kerala. This magnificent waterfall, which rises to a height of 80 feet and is encircled by thick rainforests, is a breathtaking sight, particularly during the rainy season. Athirapally's nickname comes from the water's overwhelming force and the surrounding lush flora, which evoke the majesty of Niagara Falls.

### Things to Do in Athirapally, Kerala:

- Witness the spectacular Athirapally and Vazhachal Falls
- Explore the Sholayar Forests for a refreshing nature walk
- Visit the Charpa Falls, a lesser-known yet beautiful cascade
- Enjoy a scenic drive through the lush greenery

### 11\. Kovalam – A Coastal Escape with Pristine Beaches

One of the most well-known beaches in Kerala is Kovalam, which is well-known for its crescent-shaped shoreline, swaying coconut palms, and exciting nightlife. Kovalam provides the ideal fusion of leisure, exploration, and cultural encounters thanks to its three nearby beaches: Lighthouse Beach, Hawah Beach, and Samudra Beach.

### Things to Do in Kovalam, Kerala:

- Sunbathe at Lighthouse Beach
- Enjoy water sports like surfing and parasailing
- Visit the Vizhinjam Marine Aquarium
- Experience an Ayurvedic massage at a seaside resort in Kovalam

### 12\. Trivandrum – The Capital City with a Cultural Touch

Thiruvananthapuram (Trivandrum), the capital of Kerala, seamlessly blends cultural heritage, natural beauty, and modern attractions. From centuries-old temples to serene beaches and vibrant museums, Trivandrum offers a well-rounded experience for travelers.

### Things to Do in Trivandrum, Kerala:

- Visit the iconic Padmanabhaswamy Temple
- Explore the Napier Museum and Sri Chitra Art Gallery
- Relax at Shangumugham Beach
- Take a tour of the Kerala Science and Technology Museum

### 13\. Thrissur – The Cultural Hub of Kerala

Thrissur, often called the ‘Cultural Capital of Kerala,’ is a city steeped in history and tradition. It is famous for its temples, classical arts, and vibrant festivals, particularly the grand Thrissur Pooram.

### Things to Do in Thrissur, Kerala:

- Witness the grand Thrissur Pooram festival (April-May)
- Visit Vadakkunnathan Temple
- Explore the Kerala Kalamandalam for traditional arts
- Relax at Athirappilly Waterfalls, located nearby

### 14\. Idukki – A Land of Hills and Dams

Idukki, a paradise for nature lovers, is known for its rolling hills, wildlife sanctuaries, and impressive hydroelectric projects. The region offers breathtaking views and serene landscapes, making it perfect destination in Kerala for adventure seekers and peace lovers alike.

### Things to Do in Idukki, Kerala:

- Visit the Idukki Arch Dam, India’s first and Asia’s biggest arch dam
- Explore the Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary
- Enjoy a scenic drive through the tea gardens of Idukki
- Trek to Meesapulimala for breathtaking views

### 15\. Palakkad – The Gateway to Kerala’s Natural Beauty

Palakkad, also known as the ‘Rice Bowl of Kerala,’ is famous for its lush landscapes, historical sites, and serene environment. The city serves as a vital link between Kerala and Tamil Nadu, offering a blend of cultural heritage and natural beauty.

### Things to Do in Palakkad, Kerala:

- Explore the Palakkad Fort
- Visit the Silent Valley National Park
- Take a boat ride in Malampuzha Dam
- Discover the traditional village life in Kollengode

### 16\. Kozhikode – A Blend of History and Cuisine

Kozhikode (Calicut) is a coastal city known for its rich history, beautiful beaches, and delicious Malabar cuisine. Once a major trading hub for spices in Kerala, the city offers a unique mix of colonial influences and local culture.

### Things to Do in Kozhikode, Kerala:

- Visit Kappad Beach, where Vasco da Gama landed in 1498
- Explore the Mananchira Square and Kozhikode Beach
- Taste authentic Malabar biryani
- Discover the historical Pazhassi Raja Museum

### 17\. Chinnar Wildlife Sanctuary – A Haven for Nature Lovers

Located in the rain shadow region of the Western Ghats, Chinnar Wildlife Sanctuary is one of Kerala’s most unique ecological zones, known for its dry deciduous forests, wildlife, and tribal settlements.

### Things to Do in Chinnar, Kerala:

- Go on a guided jungle safari to spot endangered species like the grizzled giant squirrel
- Trek through the scenic trails
- Visit the Thoovanam Waterfalls inside the sanctuary
- Enjoy birdwatching in the rich biodiversity zone

### 18\. Ponmudi – A Hidden Hill Station with Breathtaking Views

Ponmudi, meaning "Golden Peak," is a picturesque hill station in the Western Ghats. Known for its winding roads, lush green tea estates, and mist-covered hills, it’s a perfect getaway in Kerala for nature lovers and adventure seekers.

### Things to Do in Ponmudi, Kerala:

- Trek to Agasthyarkoodam, one of the highest peaks in Kerala
- Visit Meenmutty Waterfalls for a refreshing experience
- Enjoy a scenic drive through the 22 hairpin bends leading to Ponmudi
- Explore Peppara Wildlife Sanctuary for an offbeat adventure

### 19\. Malampuzha – The Perfect Blend of Nature and Adventure

Kerala's largest reservoir is located in the picturesque town of Malampuzha, which is part of Palakkad and offers a variety of gardens, dams, and adventure activities. Families and anyone who enjoy the outdoors will love this place.

### Things to Do in Malampuzha, Kerala:

- Visit the famous Malampuzha Dam and Gardens
- Take a ropeway ride for stunning aerial views
- Explore the Rock Garden, created by renowned artist Nek Chand
- Enjoy a boat ride in Malampuzha Reservoir

### 20\. Munroe Island – An Offbeat Backwater Destination

A hidden jewel in Kerala's backwaters, Munroe Island provides a peaceful haven with picturesque canals, verdant coconut plantations, and a traditional village way of life. It's a fantastic substitute for the busier backwater locations like Alleppey.

### Things to Do in Munroe Island, Kerala:

- Experience a serene canoe tour through the narrow canals
- Witness the unique Kallada Boat Race during the Onam festival
- Visit the historic Dutch Church
- Enjoy a traditional Kerala village stay for an authentic experience

### Best Time to Visit Kerala

Kerala is a year-round destination, but the best time to visit depends on what kind of experience you're looking for.

#### 1\. Winter (October to February) – Best Season to Visit

This is the peak tourist season in Kerala, offering pleasant weather and cool breezes, making it perfect for sightseeing, backwater cruises, and beach vacations.

**Why visit in winter?**

- Comfortable temperatures (20°C to 30°C)
- Ideal for houseboat cruises in Alleppey and Kumarakom
- Best time for hill station getaways in Munnar, Wayanad, and Thekkady
- Perfect for exploring cultural and heritage sites in Kochi and Thrissur

#### 2\. Summer (March to May) – Ideal for Hill Stations

While the coastal areas experience high humidity, hill stations in Kerala like Munnar, Vagamon, and Idukki remain pleasant. This is one of the best times to book Ayurvedic resorts and enjoy rejuvenating treatments, wellness retreats, and traditional healing practices.

**Why visit in summer?**

- Less crowded tourist spots
- Cooler temperatures in hill stations
- Great time for trekking and nature walks
- Off-season discounts on accommodations and packages

#### 3\. Monsoon (June to September) – Best for Ayurveda & Nature Lovers

Kerala receives heavy rainfall, making the landscapes lush and refreshing. It is the best time to indulge in Ayurvedic treatments and witness the beauty of the backwaters in their full glory.

**Why visit in monsoon?**

- Best time for Ayurvedic therapies and spa retreats
- Mesmerizing views of waterfalls like Athirapally and Meenmutty
- Greenest landscapes and fewer tourists
- Experience the famous Onam festival in August/September

## Conclusion

Kerala offers a wealth of breathtaking scenery, a vibrant culture, and exhilarating experiences. This lovely state offers something for everyone, whether you're searching for a wildlife adventure in Thekkady, a romantic houseboat vacation in Alleppey, or a hill station retreat in Munnar. Kerala is a must-visit location for tourists from all over the world since each of these tourist attractions delivers a distinctive experience.

So, pack your bags and get ready to explore God’s Own Country!
