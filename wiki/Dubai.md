# Dubai – Complete Travel Guide

*Source: Wikipedia - Tourist attractions in Dubai*  
*Added: August 30, 2026*  
*Country: United Arab Emirates | Region: Middle East | Nickname: "City of Gold"*

## 🏙️ Overview
Dubai is the most populous city in the United Arab Emirates and the capital of the Emirate of Dubai. Known as the "City of Gold," Dubai is a global city and business hub of the Middle East. Famous for its ultra-modern architecture, luxury shopping, and vibrant nightlife, Dubai received 14.36 million visitors in 2022. The city's economy heavily relies on tourism, with 70+ million passengers traveling through Dubai Airport annually.

## 🏆 Top Attractions

### Iconic Landmarks
- **Burj Khalifa** (⭐ Must-See) - World's tallest building at 829.8m (2,722 ft), opened in 2010 as part of Downtown Dubai. Features observation decks with panoramic city views.
- **The Dubai Frame** - 150m tall architectural landmark in Zabeel Park, offering views of old and new Dubai. Opened to public in 2018.
- **Palm Islands** - Artificial islands (Palm Jumeirah and Palm Jebel Ali) shaped like palm trees, adding 520km of beaches to the city.
- **World Islands** - Artificial archipelago of 300 islands arranged in the shape of a world map.

### Entertainment & Leisure
- **Dubai Fountain** - World's largest choreographed fountain system on 30-acre Burj Khalifa Lake. 275m long, shoots water up to 152m high with light and music shows.
- **Dubai Aquarium and Underwater Zoo** - One of the world's largest aquariums with 140+ aquatic species, including endangered species.
- **Wild Wadi Water Park** - Outdoor water park in Jumeirah with temperature-controlled wave pool, water slides, and artificial surfing machines.
- **Ski Dubai** - Indoor ski resort with 22,500 sqm of ski area, 5 slopes, and a Snow Park. Part of Mall of the Emirates.
- **Madame Tussauds Dubai** - First Tussauds museum in the Middle East on Bluewaters Island, featuring 90+ wax figures.

### Cultural & Historical Sites
- **Dubai Museum** - Located in Al Fahidi Fort (built 1787), the oldest existing building in Dubai. Displays traditional way of life before oil discovery.
- **Bastakiya District (Al Fahidi Neighbourhood)** - Historic area dating back to the 1890s, representing old Dubai with wind-tower architecture.
- **Deira Clocktower** - Historic roundabout and monument in Deira, designed by Architect Ziki Homsi in 1965.
- **Dubai Creek** - Saltwater creek that historically divided Dubai into Deira and Bur Dubai. Important for the city's commercial position and pearling industry.

### Shopping & Luxury
- **Dubai Gold Souk** - Traditional market in Deira with 300+ retailers trading in jewelry. Approximately 10 tons of gold present at any given time.
- **Mall of the Emirates** - One of the largest shopping malls in the world, home to Ski Dubai.
- **Dubai Marina** - Artificial canal city with luxury residential towers, yachting, and waterfront dining.
- **Global Village** - World's largest tourism, leisure, and entertainment project with 5+ million annual visitors.

### Modern Architecture & Business
- **Burj Al Arab** - Iconic 7-star luxury hotel standing 321m tall on a man-made island, designed to resemble a sail.
- **Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC)** - Federal financial free zone with its own legal system and courts.
- **Meydan Racecourse** - Horse racing facility with 60,000+ spectator capacity, featuring the world's richest race day (Dubai World Cup with $26.25M prize money).
- **The View at The Palm** - 360-degree observation deck at 240m high in The Palm Tower, overlooking Palm Jumeirah and the Dubai skyline.

### Resorts & Islands
- **Madinat Jumeirah** - Luxurious 5-star resort complex designed like a traditional Arabian town, with 40+ restaurants and bars.
- **Jumeirah Beach Hotel** - Iconic wave-shaped hotel next to Burj Al Arab.
- **Atlantis The Palm** - Luxury resort on Palm Jumeirah with Aquaventure Waterpark and The Lost Chambers Aquarium.

## 🌤️ Best Time to Visit
- **Winter (Nov-Mar)**: 20-30°C, pleasant weather, peak tourist season ⭐
- **Spring (Apr-May)**: 30-40°C, hot but manageable
- **Summer (Jun-Sep)**: 35-45°C+, extremely hot, indoor activities recommended
- **Autumn (Oct-Nov)**: 30-35°C, good for outdoor exploration

## 🚗 Getting Around
- **Metro**: Dubai Metro (Red, Green, Blue lines) - modern, air-conditioned
- **Taxis**: Readily available, metered, and relatively affordable
- **Buses**: Extensive network covering most areas
- **Tram**: Dubai Tram in Marina area
- **Abra (Boat)**: Traditional boat rides across Dubai Creek (AED 1)
- **Ride-hailing**: Careem, Uber available
- **Self-Drive**: Car rental available, drive on the right

## 🛍️ Shopping
- **The Dubai Mall** - World's largest shopping mall by total land area
- **Mall of the Emirates** - Features Ski Dubai
- **Ibn Battuta Mall** - Themed shopping mall with sections representing different countries
- **Gold Souk** - Traditional gold market
- **Spice Souk** - Market for spices, perfumes, and textiles

## 🍽️ Cuisine

### Must-Try Dishes
- **Local Emirati**: Machboos, Luqaimat, Harees, Balaleet
- **Middle Eastern**: Shawarma, Falafel, Hummus, Tabbouleh
- **International**: Wide variety from Indian, Pakistani, Filipino, to fine dining
- **Street Food**: Samosas, Manakish, Al Harees
- **Sweets**: Baklava, Kunafa, Date-filled pastries

### Dining Experiences
- **At.mosphere** - Restaurant on the 122nd floor of Burj Khalifa
- **Al Mahara** - Underwater restaurant at Burj Al Arab
- **Pierchic** - Overwater dining with Burj Al Arab views
- **Zeta Seventy Seven** - Award-winning restaurant with panoramic views

## 🏨 Accommodation
- **Luxury**: Burj Al Arab, Atlantis The Palm, Armani Hotel, One&Only The Palm
- **Business**: JW Marriott Marquis, Four Seasons, Grand Hyatt
- **Mid-Range**: Rove Hotels, 25hours Hotel, The Westin
- **Budget**: Ibis, Holiday Inn, Premier Inn
