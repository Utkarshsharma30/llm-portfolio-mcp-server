## 🌏 Overview

**Asia** is the world's largest continent by both land area and population, home to **4.6 billion people** (more than half of the world's population). The continent is so massive and diverse that it cannot be conceptualized as a single travel destination. Instead, it offers an incredible variety of experiences:

- **Desert ruins and modern mega-malls** of the Middle East
- **Magnificent ancient monuments and giant mountains** of South Asia
- **Beach bungalows and jungle treks** of Southeast Asia
- **Mega-cities and technology capitals** of East Asia

Asia receives a tremendous number of travelers for both tourism and business. In 2025, **8 of the world's 10 most-visited cities** were in Asia: Bangkok, Hong Kong, London, Macau, Istanbul, Dubai, Mecca, Antalya, Paris, and Kuala Lumpur.

**Key Fact**: Asia contains virtually every kind of climate and ecosystem that exists in the world, and a corresponding diversity of plants and animals.

## 🗺️ Regions of Asia

Asia is divided into several distinct regions, each with its own unique characteristics:

### 1. 🏔️ Caucasus
**Countries**: Abkhazia, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia, South Ossetia
- **Description**: Mountainous range connecting Europe and the Middle East
- **Highlights**: Ancient heritage, dramatic landscapes, unique cultures
- **Related**: [[Georgia]]

### 2. 🏜️ Central Asia
**Countries**: Afghanistan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan
- **Description**: The heart of the historic Silk Road
- **Highlights**: Spectacular landscapes (deserts to mountains), ancient mosques, Soviet architecture
- **Key Cities**: Tashkent, Bukhara, Samarkand
- **Famous Route**: [[Silk Road]]

### 3. 🏯 East Asia
**Countries**: China (mainland), Hong Kong, Japan, Macau, Mongolia, North Korea, South Korea, Taiwan
- **Description**: The perfect dichotomy of old versus new
- **Highlights**: Mega-cities at the forefront of technology, well-preserved ancient temples, sites of ancient cultures and philosophies
- **Key Cities**: Beijing, Tokyo, Seoul, Shanghai, Hong Kong
- **Natural Features**: Vast open plains, rural landscapes

### 4. 🕌 Middle East
**Countries**: Bahrain, Iran, Iraq, Israel, Jordan, Kuwait, Lebanon, Oman, Palestinian territories, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Syria, Turkey, United Arab Emirates, Yemen
- **Description**: Home of one of the world's oldest civilizations and the birthplace of three Abrahamic religions
- **Highlights**: Fast-growing region with rich heritage, ancient ruins, modern development
- **Key Cities**: Dubai, Istanbul, Jerusalem, Tehran
- **Religious Sites**: Holy Land (Israel/Palestine)

### 5. ❄️ Russia (Asian Part)
**Regions**: Urals, Siberia, Russian Far East
- **Description**: Northern Asia, traditionally known as Siberia
- **Highlights**: Vast, sparsely populated region from Arctic to Central Asian deserts
- **Famous Railway**: [[Trans-Siberian Railway]]

### 6. 🏔️ South Asia ⭐ *Focus of this Wiki*
**Countries**: Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan, Sri Lanka
- **Description**: Breathtaking Himalayas in the north, tropical humid waterways in the south, lively chaotic cities and many ancient historical sites
- **Highlights**: Religious diversity, rich history, diverse landscapes
- **Key Countries in this Wiki**:
  - [[India]] - Comprehensive guide with 50+ destinations
  - [[Mumbai]] - Financial capital with 10+ attractions
  - [[Goa]] - Beach paradise with 15+ beaches
  - [[wiki/Kerala]] - "God's Own Country" with 25+ attractions
  - [[Maharashtra]] - Mumbai's state with 20+ attractions
  - [[Manali]] - Himalayan hill station

### 7. 🏝️ Southeast Asia
**Countries**: Brunei, Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Timor-Leste, Vietnam
- **Description**: Hot and humid, Buddhist monasteries, tropical beaches, bustling streetlife
- **Highlights**: Relaxed getaways from rowdy cities, diverse religious sites
- **Key Destinations**: Bangkok, Singapore, Bali, Angkor Wat, Vietnam
- **Backpacker Trail**: Banana Pancake Trail

## ✈️ Getting to Asia

### By Plane
Asia's busiest airports (2026):
- **Hong Kong (HKG)** - Hong Kong International Airport
- **Dubai (DXB)** - Dubai International Airport
- **Beijing (PEK, PKX)** - Beijing Capital & Daxing Airports
- **Singapore (SIN)** - Singapore Changi Airport
- **Kuala Lumpur (KUL)** - Kuala Lumpur International Airport
- **Bangkok (BKK)** - Suvarnabhumi Airport
- **Seoul (ICN)** - Incheon International Airport

- **Jakarta (CGK)** - Soekarno-Hatta International Airport
- **Mumbai (BOM)** - Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport ([[Mumbai]])
- **Bangalore (BLR)** - Kempegowda International Airport

**Tip**: Many of these airports are among the best equipped in the world, known for efficient service and ample distractions for long layovers.

### By Train
- **Trans-Siberian Railway** - Connects European Russia with the Russian Far East
  - Routes: Moscow to Vladivostok, Trans-Mongolian (via Ulaanbaatar), Trans-Manchurian (via Beijing)
- **From Europe**: Enter through Russia or Turkey
- **Within Asia**: High-speed rail networks in China, Japan, South Korea, India, and Taiwan

### By Boat
- **Cruise destinations**: Various companies offer cruises from Australia, North America
- **Freighter travel**: Options to Mediterranean Asian ports
- **Ferry services**: Connect China, South Korea, Japan; Southeast Asian islands
- **Istanbul ferries**: Cross the Bosphorus (Europe-Asia border)

### On Foot
- **Yekaterinburg** (Russia) - City straddling Europe-Asia border, walk across
- **Istanbul** (Turkey) - Straddles continents, but pedestrian crossing restricted

## 🚆 Getting Around Asia

### By Plane
- **Advantages**: Fast, covers vast distances efficiently
- **Cost**: Lower average fares than Europe or America
- **Airlines**:
  - **Full-service**: Singapore Airlines, Cathay Pacific, Korean Air, Asiana, ANA, JAL, EVA Air
  - **Middle Eastern**: Emirates, Qatar Airways, Etihad Airways
  - **Low-cost**: Rapidly expanding networks, especially in Southeast Asia

### By Train
**Highly developed networks in**:
- [[India]] - Extensive rail network
- China - World's largest high-speed rail network
- South Korea (KTX) - Modern high-speed lines
- Japan (Shinkansen) - Efficient bullet trains
- Taiwan - High-speed rail

**Scenic routes**:
- **Trans-Siberian Railway** (Russia)
- **Konkan Railway** ([[India]]) - Coastal route
- **Eastern & Oriental Express** (Luxury trains)

### By Boat
- **Ferry connections**: China-South Korea-Japan, Southeast Asian islands
- **Houseboats**: Backwaters of [[wiki/Kerala]], [[India]]
- **Cruises**: Various options for island hopping

### By Bus
- **Range**: From luxurious buses to "chicken buses" (old, crowded)
- **Budget**: Excellent for budget-conscious travelers
- **Experience**: Good way to meet locals

### By Car
- **Challenges**: High population density, traffic congestion
- **Requirements**: International driving permit, Carnet de Passages (for vehicle)
- **Note**: Mainland China does NOT accept international driver's licenses

### Local Transport
- **Taxis**: Widely available in cities
- **Auto-rickshaws/Tuk-tuks**: [[India]], Thailand, Philippines
- **Jeepneys**: Philippines
- **Songthaews**: Thailand
- **Marshrutkas**: Ex-Soviet states
- **Metro systems**: Modern systems in many major cities (Tokyo, Shanghai, Seoul, Singapore, Mumbai)

## 🍽️ Asian Cuisine

Asia offers **incredibly diverse cuisine**, from Turkish kebabs to Arabic pita bread to Indian curries and Chinese noodles. Rice is a very common staple throughout the continent.

### Regional Cuisines
| Region | Key Dishes | Highlights |
|--------|------------|------------|
| **Middle East** | Kebabs, pita, hummus, falafel | Rich spices, grilled meats |
| **South Asia** | Curry, biryani, dosa, naan | Spicy, flavorful, vegetarian options |
| **East Asia** | Noodles, dumplings, sushi, hot pot | Balance of flavors, fresh ingredients |
| **Southeast Asia** | Pad Thai, pho, satay, laksa | Tropical fruits, street food culture |

**From this Wiki**:
- [[India]] - Comprehensive guide to Indian cuisine
- [[wiki/Kerala]] - Unique coconut-based cuisine
- [[Mumbai]] - Street food capital (Vada Pav, Bhel Puri, Pav Bhaji)
- [[Goa]] - Seafood and Portuguese-influenced dishes
- [[Maharashtra]] - Vada Pav, Misal Pav, Puran Poli

### Dining Etiquette
- **Chopsticks**: Used in East Asia and parts of Southeast Asia
- **Hands**: Common in South Asia (right hand only)
- **Cutlery**: Available in hotels/restaurants with Western food
- **Vegetarian**: Many options, especially in [[India]]

### Must-Try Drinks
- **Tea**: Most common beverage in South and East Asia
- **Fresh fruit juices**: Tropical areas
- **Sugar cane juice**: Popular in Indian cities
- **Lassi**: Yogurt-based drink ([[India]], Pakistan)
- **Doogh**: Yogurt drink (Iran, Armenia)
- **Rice wine**: Japan, Korea, China

## ⚠️ Stay Safe in Asia

### Armed Conflicts & No-Go Areas
**AVOID - High Risk**:
- Afghanistan - Ongoing conflict
- Myanmar - Civil war
- Yemen - Full-scale civil war
- Parts of Pakistan (Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, FATA)
- Parts of Iraq

**CAUTION - Check Conditions**:
- Gaza Strip (Israel/Palestine) - Active conflict
- Syria - Fragile state, periodic attacks
- Iran - War with US (as of July 2026)
- Lebanon, West Bank - Political instability
- North Caucasus (Russia) - Insurgent groups

**Generally Safe**:
- **East Asia**: Safest region for visitors
- **Southeast Asia**: Mostly safe (except Myanmar, southern Thailand provinces)
- **South Asia**: Generally safe (except conflict zones)
- **India**: Safe in most areas, Himachal Pradesh & Uttarakhand safest for solo female travelers

### Crime
- **General**: Varies by country and region
- **Precaution**: Always know embassy/consulate addresses
- **Scams**: Common in tourist areas
- **Pickpocketing**: Be cautious in crowded places

### Natural Disasters
Asia experiences virtually every type of natural disaster:
- **Earthquakes**: Silk Road areas, Pacific Ring of Fire
- **Volcanoes**: Japan, Philippines, Indonesia, Kamchatka
- **Floods**: South and Southeast Asia (monsoon rains)
- **Tropical storms**: Typhoons (E/SE Asia), cyclones (Indian Ocean)
- **Smog**: Northern China, air pollution

## 💊 Stay Healthy in Asia

### Tropical Diseases
- **Risk Areas**: Southeast Asia, South Asia (humid tropical regions)
- **Prevention**: Vaccinations, mosquito protection, safe food/water
- **Common**: Malaria, dengue, typhoid, cholera

### Cold Weather
- **Risk Areas**: Mongolia, Siberia, Northern China
- **Challenges**: Extremely harsh winters
- **Preparation**: Proper winter clothing, emergency supplies

### Altitude Sickness
- **Risk Areas**: Tibet, Himalayas, parts of Central Asia
- **Prevention**: Acclimatize gradually, stay hydrated
- **Treatment**: Descend immediately if symptoms occur

## 🙏 Respect & Cultural Sensitivity

### Religion
- **Middle East, South Asia, Southeast Asia**: Religion taken very seriously
- **Rules**: Follow dress codes, photography restrictions, sacred object handling
- **Consequences**: Sanctions can be severe for violations

### Dress Code
- **General Rule**: Dress modestly
- **Modest clothing**: Pants covering knees, shirts with sleeves
- **Strict countries**: May require women to wear veils
- **Temples/Religious Sites**: Cover shoulders and knees
- **Beaches**: Swimwear acceptable on beaches, cover up when leaving

- **Best connectivity**: South Korea, Japan, Singapore, many other countries
