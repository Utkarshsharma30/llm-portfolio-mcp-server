## 🎯 Trip Planning

### By Duration
- **1 Week**: One region done well (e.g., Rajasthan OR Kerala)
- **10 Days**: Two connected regions (e.g., Jaipur + Rajasthan)
- **2 Weeks**: Four to six places with depth (e.g., North loop + South)
- **3+ Weeks**: Multiple regions with deep exploration

### By Interest
- **Adventure Travel**: Trekking, skiing, paragliding, rafting
- **Cultural Heritage**: Temples, forts, palaces, museums
- **Spiritual Journeys**: Yoga retreats, pilgrimage sites, ashrams
- **Wildlife & Nature**: National parks, wildlife sanctuaries
- **Food & Cuisine**: Regional specialties and culinary experiences
- **Beach & Relaxation**: Coastal destinations and island getaways

### Best Time to Visit (India)
- **October to March**: Best for most of India (cool and dry)
- **April to June**: Hot, but good for hill stations
- **July to September**: Monsoon season (lush but rainy)
- **Winter (Dec-Feb)**: Perfect for snow activities in Himalayas

### Best Time to Visit (Asia)
- **Winter**: South Asia, Southeast Asia (dry season)
- **Spring/Fall**: East Asia, Central Asia (mild temperatures)
- **Avoid**: Monsoon seasons, extreme heat periods
