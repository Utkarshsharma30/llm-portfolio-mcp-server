# Singapore – Complete Travel Guide

*Source: Never Ending Footsteps - 26 Incredible Things to Do in Singapore*  
*Added: August 30, 2026*  
*Country: Singapore | Region: Southeast Asia | Nickname: "The Lion City"*

## 🌆 Overview
Singapore is a sovereign island city-state in Southeast Asia, known for its cleanliness, efficiency, and strict laws. Once considered "boring" by backpackers, Singapore has transformed into one of the world's most exciting destinations. It's a country where everything "just works" - safe, organized, English-speaking, and incredibly easy to navigate. Singapore offers a unique blend of Chinese, Malay, Indian, and Western cultures, creating a rich multicultural experience.

## 🌿 Nature & Gardens

### Gardens by the Bay
- **Supertree Grove** - Iconic 25-50 meter tall tree-like structures that dominate the Gardens' landscape
- **Cloud Forest** - Massive greenhouse featuring a 35-meter tall mountain covered in lush vegetation and the world's tallest indoor waterfall
- **Flower Dome** - Largest glass greenhouse in the world, showcasing plants from five different continents
- **OCBC Skyway** - Elevated walkway connecting the Supertrees, offering stunning aerial views
- **Floral Fantasy** - Indoor attraction with four themed gardens featuring floating floral displays

**Garden Rhapsody** - Free nightly light-and-music show that shouldn't be missed. The Supertrees come alive with synchronized lights and music.

### Singapore Botanic Gardens
- **UNESCO World Heritage Site** - The only tropical garden on UNESCO's World Heritage list
- **National Orchid Garden** (S$15 entry) - Home to 1,500+ orchid species and 3,000+ hybrids
- **Free Entry** - Unlike most Singapore attractions, the main gardens are completely free
- **Highlights**: Ginger Garden (100+ ginger species), Swan Lake, Rainforest Trail, Healing Garden, Heliconia Walk, Bandstand
- **Hours**: 5am to midnight daily

## 🏙️ Iconic Landmarks

### Marina Bay Sands
- **SkyPark Observation Deck** - Panoramic views of the city from 200m above ground (S$26)
- **Infinity Pool** - World's largest infinity pool at 150m, exclusive to hotel guests
- **ArtScience Museum** - Lotus-shaped museum with fascinating exhibitions
- **Sunset Cocktails** - Spectacular views with drinks at CE LA VI or other rooftop bars

### Sentosa Island
- **Universal Studios Singapore** - Movie theme park with 24 attractions
- **S.E.A. Aquarium** - One of the world's largest aquariums
- **Adventure Cove Waterpark** - Marine life-themed water park
- **Palawan Beach** - Southernmost point of continental Asia
- **iFly Singapore** - Indoor skydiving
- **Megazip Adventure Park** - Zipline and treetop courses

### Merlion Park
- **Merlion Statue** - Iconic half-lion, half-fish statue, symbol of Singapore
- **Marina Bay Waterfront** - Perfect spot for photos with the city skyline

### Singapore Flyer
- **Observation Wheel** - 165m tall, offering 360-degree views of the city
- **Duration**: 30 minutes per rotation

## 🏛️ Culture & Heritage

### Chinatown
- **Buddha Tooth Relic Temple** - Stunning Buddhist temple housing what is believed to be a tooth of Buddha
- **Chinatown Heritage Centre** - Museum showcasing the history of Chinese immigrants
- **Maxwell Food Centre** - Famous for local hawker food

### Kampong Glam
- **Sultan Mosque** - Beautiful gold-domed mosque, a landmark of the Muslim community
- **Haji Lane** - Colorful street with boutique shops, cafes, and street art
- **Arab Street** - Traditional textile and perfume shops

### Little India
- **Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple** - Oldest Hindu temple in Singapore
- **Tekka Centre** - Hawker centre with authentic Indian food
- **Mustafa Centre** - 24-hour department store selling everything

### Kampong Glam Conservation Area
- **Traditional Malay Houses** - Colorful two-storey shophouses with ornate facades
- **Cultural Experience** - Malay culture, food, and heritage

## 🍽️ Food & Dining

### Hawker Centres (Must-Visit)
- **Chinatown Complex Food Centre** - Largest hawker centre with 200+ stalls
- **Maxwell Food Centre** - Famous for Tian Tian Hainanese Chicken Rice
- **Lau Pa Sat** - Historic market in a beautiful Victorian-style building
- **Old Airport Road Food Centre** - Huge variety of local dishes
- **Tiong Bahru Market** - Trendy area with great food options

### Must-Try Dishes
- **Chicken Rice** - Hainanese chicken with fragrant rice (S$3-5)
- **Chilli Crab** - Whole mud crab stir-fried in sweet, spicy tomato-based sauce
- **Laksa** - Noodle soup with rich coconut curry broth
- **Char Kway Teow** - Stir-fried flat rice noodles with egg and vegetables
- **Satay** - Skewered and grilled meat served with peanut sauce
- **Hainanese Curry Rice** - Steamed rice with curry and various toppings
- **Ice Kachang** - Shaved ice dessert with red beans and jelly
- **Kaya Toast** - Toast with coconut jam and butter, served with soft-boiled eggs

### Fine Dining
- **Odette** - 3-Michelin-starred French restaurant
- **Zen** - Nordic fine dining
- **Jaan by Kirk Westaway** - British-French cuisine
- **Burnt Ends** - Modern Australian barbecue

## 🌤️ Best Time to Visit
- **Year-Round**: Singapore's tropical climate means warm weather (25-35°C) all year
- **Dry Season (Feb-Apr)**: Slightly less humidity, most comfortable
- **Monsoon (Nov-Jan)**: Heavy but short rain showers
- **Avoid**: Chinese New Year (Jan/Feb) and Christmas - peak tourist season with higher prices

## 💰 Estimated Costs (per day)
- **Budget Backpacker**: S$70-100 ($50-75 USD) - Hostels, hawker food, public transport
- **Mid-Range**: S$150-250 ($110-185 USD) - 3-star hotels, mix of hawker and restaurant meals
- **Luxury**: S$400+ ($300+ USD) - 5-star hotels, fine dining, attractions

**Note**: Singapore is one of the most expensive cities in Southeast Asia, but hawker food offers great value.

## 🚗 Getting Around
- **MRT (Mass Rapid Transit)**: Efficient, clean, and air-conditioned subway system
- **Buses**: Extensive network, air-conditioned, and affordable (S$0.90-2.00 per trip)
- **Taxis**: Metered, reliable, and relatively affordable (Grab app recommended)
- **Ride-hailing**: Grab (like Uber), GoJek available
- **Walking**: Many attractions are within walking distance in the city center
- **Tourist Passes**: Singapore Tourist Pass for unlimited MRT/bus rides

## 💡 Travel Tips
1. **Tap Water is Safe** - Singapore's tap water is perfectly safe to drink
2. **No Tipping Culture** - Service charge is usually included in bills
3. **Strict Laws** - No chewing gum, littering, or jaywalking (fines are steep)
4. **Cashless Society** - Credit cards widely accepted, but keep some cash for hawker centres
5. **Free Attractions** - Gardens by the Bay (outdoor areas), Botanic Gardens, Merlion Park, Haw Par Villa
6. **Sentosa Entry** - Free to enter Sentosa Island, but attractions have individual fees
7. **Singapore Sling** - Try the national cocktail at Raffles Hotel's Long Bar
