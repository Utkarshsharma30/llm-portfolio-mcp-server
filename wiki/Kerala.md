# Kerala – Complete Travel Guide

*Source: Top Indian Places to Visit Dataset*  
*Added: August 30, 2026*  
*Nickname: "God's Own Country" | State: Kerala | Region: Southern India*

## 🌴 Overview
Kerala is one of India's most popular tourist destinations, renowned for its serene backwaters, lush greenery, beautiful beaches, rich cultural heritage, and unique cuisine. The state has the highest literacy rate in India and excellent healthcare.

## 🏝️ Top Attractions by Region

### Kochi (Gateway to Kerala)
- **Fort Kochi** (⭐ 4.4) - Historic area, Chinese Fishing Nets, Mattancherry Palace
- **Kerala Folklore Museum** (⭐ 4.4) - 5000+ artifacts, traditional architecture
- **Wonderla Amusement Park** (⭐ 4.6) - Rides, water park, entertainment

### Alappuzha (Alleppey)
- **Alappuzha Beach** (⭐ 4.5) - Pier, lighthouse, children's park
- **Houseboat Stays** - Backwaters cruise, unique Kerala experience

### Munnar
- **Munnar Tea Gardens** (⭐ 4.3) - Rolling hills of tea bushes
- **Eravikulam National Park** (⭐ 4.5) - Nilgiri Tahr, Neelakurinji flowers

### Thiruvananthapuram (Trivandrum)
- **Padmanabhaswamy Temple** (⭐ 4.7) - Wealthiest temple, Lord Vishnu

### Wayanad
- **Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary** (⭐ 4.5) - Elephants, tigers, biodiversity

### Thekkady
- **Periyar National Park** (⭐ 4.3) - Elephants, boat safari, tiger reserve

### Kumarakom
- **Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary** (⭐ 3.8) - 400+ bird species, mangrove forests
- **Houseboats** - Luxury stays on backwaters

### Other Notable Spots
- **Varkala Beach** (⭐ 4.6) - Cliff beach, natural springs
- **Bekal Fort** (⭐ 4.5) - Large coastal fort
- **Kovalam Beach** (⭐ 4.4) - Three crescent-shaped beaches

## 🚤 Backwaters of Kerala
The backwaters are a network of lakes, lagoons, and canals. **Houseboat cruises** are the highlight:
- **Types**: Luxury (₹8,000-20,000/night), Deluxe (₹5,000-8,000), Budget (₹2,000-5,000)
- **Duration**: 1 night/2 days (most popular), day cruises
- **Routes**: Alleppey to Kumarakom, Alleppey to Kottayam
- **Experience**: Traditional Kerala cuisine, village life, sunset views

## 🌿 Wildlife Sanctuaries
- **Periyar** (Thekkady) - Elephants, tigers, boat safari
- **Wayanad** - Elephants, bison, waterfalls
- **Silent Valley** - Lion-tailed macaque
- **Eravikulam** (Munnar) - Nilgiri Tahr
- **Parambikulam** - Tigers, elephants
- **Chinnar** - Gaur, Giant Grizzled Squirrel

## 🎭 Culture & Festivals

### Classical Arts
- **Kathakali** - Elaborate costumes, detailed gestures
- **Mohiniyattam** - Graceful dance by women
- **Koodiyattam** - Oldest theatrical tradition (UNESCO)
- **Theyyam** - Ritual dance with costumes
- **Thirayattam** - Festival of goddesses

### Major Festivals
- **Onam** (Aug-Sep) - Harvest festival, boat races, sadya feast
- **Thrissur Pooram** (Apr-May) - Elephant procession, fireworks
- **Nehru Trophy Boat Race** (Aug) - Snake boat race
- **Vishu** (April) - New Year, Vishu Kani
- **Thiruvathira** (Dec-Jan) - Festival of Shiva and Parvati

## 🍛 Kerala Cuisine

### Must-Try Dishes
- **Rice & Bread**: Appam, Dosa, Idiyappam, Puttu, Idli
- **Curries**: Sadya (vegetarian feast), Fish Curry, Prawn Curry, Beef Fry
- **Seafood**: Karimeen Pollichathu, Chemmeen Curry, Meen Moilee
- **Breakfast**: Upma, Poha, Ada Pradhaman
- **Snacks**: Banana Chips, Jackfruit Chips, Tapioca Chips
- **Sweets**: Payasam, Palada Pradhaman, Neyyappam, Unniyappam
- **Drinks**: Tender Coconut Water, Sulaimani, Karkidaka Kanji

## 🍃 Ayurveda & Wellness
Kerala is the birthplace of Ayurveda. Popular treatments:
- **Panchakarma** - 5-step detoxification (7-28 days)
- **Abhyanga** - Full-body oil massage
- **Shirodhara** - Warm oil flow on forehead
- **Pizhichil** - Warm oil treatment with massage
- **Njavarakizhi** - Rice and milk bolus massage

**Best Centers**: Kairali (Palakkad), Somatheeram (Kovalam), Punarnava (Trivandrum)

## 🌤️ Best Time to Visit
- **Winter (Oct-Feb)**: 20-32°C, pleasant, ideal for sightseeing ⭐
- **Summer (Mar-May)**: 25-35°C, hot, good for hill stations
- **Monsoon (Jun-Sep)**: Heavy rains, lush greenery, best for Ayurveda

## 🚗 Getting Around
- **Airports**: Cochin (COK), Trivandrum (TRV), Calicut (CCJ)
- **Trains**: Good connectivity to major Indian cities
- **Buses**: KSRTC (Volvo, ordinary)
- **Local**: Auto-rickshaws, taxis, ferries

## 🏨 Accommodation Types
- **Houseboats**: 1-4 bedrooms, traditional kettuvallam
- **Resorts**: Luxury, Ayurveda, backwater, beach, hill
- **Heritage**: Palaces, tharavads, bungalows
- **Homestays**: Family, farmstays, plantation stays
- **Budget**: Hostels, guesthouses, lodges
