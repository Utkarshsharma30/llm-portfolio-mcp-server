*Source: Compiled from multiple travel datasets*  
*Added: August 30, 2026*  
*Total Attractions: 326 across India*

## 📊 Dataset Overview

This comprehensive dataset contains **326 major tourist attractions** across **India**, organized by zones, states, and cities. Each attraction includes detailed information such as:

- **Name** and **Type** (Monument, Temple, Beach, Museum, etc.)
- **Location** (City, State, Zone)
- **Rating** (⭐ scale based on visitor reviews)
- **Entrance Fee** (for Indians and Foreigners where applicable)
- **Time Required** (estimated visiting duration)
- **Best Time to Visit** (seasonal recommendations)
- **Key Features** and **Highlights**

The dataset is structured to help travelers plan their itineraries based on regions, interests, and travel preferences.

## 🗓️ Best Time to Visit by Region

### North India
- **Winter (Oct-Mar)**: Ideal for Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh
- **Summer (Apr-Jun)**: Good for hill stations (Shimla, Manali, Nainital)
- **Monsoon (Jul-Sep)**: Lush greenery, but avoid travel in plains

### South India
- **Winter (Dec-Feb)**: Best for Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh
- **Summer (Mar-May)**: Hot, but good for hill stations (Ooty, Kodaikanal)
- **Monsoon (Jun-Sep)**: Kerala at its best, but heavy rains

### West India
- **Winter (Nov-Feb)**: Best for Mumbai, Goa, Maharashtra
- **Summer (Mar-May)**: Hot, but hill stations (Lonavala, Mahabaleshwar) are pleasant
- **Monsoon (Jun-Sep)**: Coastal areas receive heavy rainfall

### East & Northeast India
- **Winter (Nov-Feb)**: Best for West Bengal, Odisha
- **Summer (Mar-May)**: Hot and humid
- **Monsoon (Jun-Sep)**: Heavy rainfall, especially in Northeast
